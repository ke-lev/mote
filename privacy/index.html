mote and your privacy

mote has no account, no ads and no analytics. its maker runs no server and never sees anything about you.

where you are

mote asks for your location to show the sky over you. it sends that place, rounded to about a kilometre, to apple’s weather service to fetch the forecast, and to apple to learn the name of your town. if you say no, mote shows the sky over the city your time zone is named for.

if you pick a place for a widget, what you type is sent to apple maps to find it.

what stays on your phone

the last place mote read a sky for, the last forecast, and whether you have seen how to use it. deleting mote deletes them.
